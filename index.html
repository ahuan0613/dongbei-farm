跟T&T或Superstore的玉米有什么区别？ · Different from supermarket corn?

超市卖的是甜玉米——脆、水、甜。粘苞米是白糯玉米——黏、糯、香，玉米味浓但不齁甜。两种不同的品种，就像寿司米和印度香米的区别。东北人都懂。

Supermarket corn is sweet corn — crunchy, watery, sugary. Sticky corn is a waxy variety — chewy, savory, aromatic. Two completely different species.

转基因吗？ · Is this GMO?

不是。白糯玉米是东北传统品种，种了好几代人，非转基因。

No. Traditional white waxy corn, grown for generations. Non-GMO.
